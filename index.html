Aerospace engineering, one short at a time

Aerospace Engineering Explainer is an independent educational channel. It publishes short clips that explain how aircraft, rockets, spacecraft and drones work: propulsion, aerodynamics, flight dynamics and control, structures and space systems.

Watch on YouTube   Instagram

How the clips are made

Clips come only from Creative Commons (CC BY) lectures and talks, and NASA public-domain media. Every clip credits its source on screen and in its description. NASA clips are credited "NASA"; this channel is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

About the posting tool (ClipBot)

ClipBot is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the owner's own clips to the owner's own YouTube channel, and the Instagram Graph API to post them to the owner's own Instagram account. It does not access any other person's account or data.
